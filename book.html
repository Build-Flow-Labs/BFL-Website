The Missing Manual for Supply Chain Security

Your SBOM lists the ingredients. But who verified the kitchen was clean? Pipeline Trust is the field guide for engineering leaders and security practitioners who need to move beyond compliance theater to actual, verifiable integrity.

Based on real-world implementations, peer-reviewed research, and hard lessons from production incidents. This book bridges the gap between academic frameworks and the messy reality of securing modern CI/CD.
